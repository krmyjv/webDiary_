First Year Experience

My first year in college was full of excitement, challenges, and unforgettable experiences. Entering the world of Information Technology made me nervous at first, but I slowly adapted and learned many new things with my classmates and friends.

Programming Journey

During my first semester, I learned the basics of programming and computer systems. Subjects like Introduction to Computing and Computer Programming helped me understand how technology works and improved my logical thinking skills.

Networking and Databases

In my second year, I explored more advanced topics like Networking, Information Management, and Human Computer Interaction. These subjects helped me understand how systems communicate and how databases are managed.

Application Development

One of the most exciting parts of my journey was learning Application Development. Creating simple websites and systems made me more motivated to become a web developer in the future.

Sportsfest 2026

Aside from academics, I also enjoyed participating in school activities. The Sportsfest 2026 became one of the best memories of my college life. Seeing our department win and celebrating with friends was unforgettable.
